FormatSend it to

Every file renders with no fonts installed, so a printer who owns nothing still gets the right letters. The typeface is free in any case.

Digital print, not screen print

Over two thousand colours cannot be separated into spot inks. For fabric, ask for DTG or DTF.

Convert to CMYK for litho

The files are sRGB. Ask the printer to convert and send a proof. The golds are the ones to check.
