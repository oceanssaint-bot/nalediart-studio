The marks

Which mark, when

Four of the seventeen do almost all the work.

Primary

The default. Website header, invoices, proposals, anywhere with room for two lines.

Badge

Stickers, packaging tape, wax seals, the back of a canvas. Carries Durban in the ring.

Submark

Social avatar, favicon, app icon. Anywhere the name is already beside it.

Horizontal

Letterhead, email footer, a wide banner, wherever height is short and width is not.
