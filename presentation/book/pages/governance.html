Running it

Who decides

One source, three checks, and a person to ask.

One source

Every mark comes from one master artwork file. Anything made from a screenshot, a website image or a forwarded message is not the logo.

Sending it out

Printers and signwriters get the PDF or SVG. Anyone designing gets the SVG and this book. Nobody needs the PNG unless they ask.

Before it goes out

Check three things: the mark is not stretched, it has its clear space, and the gold is the deep one if it is being read as text.

When in doubt

Ask before improvising. A five-minute question is cheaper than a reprint, and far cheaper than a sign.
