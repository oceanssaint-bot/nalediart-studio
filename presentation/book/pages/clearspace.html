Room and floor

How much space it needs, and how small it may go.

Measure the height of a capital N in the mark. Keep that much empty on every side: no type, no photograph edge, no border. For the round marks, keep a quarter of the ring diameter.

Below these, use a simpler mark

The artwork carries fine detail. Under these sizes the sunflowers stop reading as sunflowers and turn to noise. That is when to switch to the submark or the plain-ring wordmark.
