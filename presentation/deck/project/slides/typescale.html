Rules that hold it together

Emphasise with weight, italic or colour, never by inventing a sixth size. Labels are uppercase with generous letter-spacing; nothing else is uppercase. Body text stops at about 70 characters a line. One typeface throughout, so nothing can drift apart and there is no licence to manage.
