The diagnosis

Three problems

Not one problem, and none of them the painting’s fault.

01

The cut-out failed

Whoever removed the background left a grey halo around every edge. On white you barely notice. On anything else it shows as a dirty outline.

02

Nothing to enlarge

418 pixels is a thumbnail. Enough for a small website image, nowhere near enough for a banner, a vinyl sign or a printed catalogue.

03

Pieces were missing

The hair and several sunflowers stopped where the eraser stopped, not where the painting did. Shapes ended mid-petal.

Naledi Art Studio · Brand identity

06

Worth saying plainly: none of this was a fault of the painting. The painting was fine. Everything here happened to it afterwards, in the handling.
