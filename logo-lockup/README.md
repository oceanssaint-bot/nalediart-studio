## The type

- **Gotham Book**, letterspaced and converted to outlines.
- **Colour #3B342E**, a warm dark brown that sits with the seed heads and the
  deep skin tones in the painting.
- **No fonts are referenced in the files.** The letters are shapes, so nothing
  reflows or substitutes on another machine, at a printer, or in Canva. Verified
  by rendering with all system fonts disabled.

The proportions come from your original painted lettering, measured off
`IMG_0902 (1).jpeg`:

| | Original painting | This logo |
|---|---|---|
| Main line width | 1.46× the artwork width | 1.46× |
| Letterspacing | 0.32em | 0.32em |
| Stroke weight | 6.1% of cap height | 11.3% |

Only the stroke weight was deliberately increased. At 6% the original lettering
was a hairline, which is why it washed out at small sizes and from a distance.

"STUDIO" is set smaller and opened up to 0.74em so it reads as a second tier
rather than competing with the name.

## Using it

- **Minimum size: about 180px wide** for the full lockup. Below that "STUDIO"
  starts to thin out — use the artwork on its own instead.
- **Dark backgrounds:** use the reversed version. On mid-tone colours (a teal, a
  mustard) the brown still reads but is muted; the reversed version has more
  punch if you want the name to carry.
- **Printing:** the artwork is a painting with ~2,200 colours, so ask for DTG,
  DTF or digital printing rather than spot-colour screen printing. The files are
  sRGB and a printer will convert to CMYK; expect the gold and pink to shift a
  little.
- **Clear space:** the files already carry a margin of about 6% of the artwork
  width. Keep at least that much clear of other elements.

## Licensing note

Gotham is a commercial typeface (Hoefler & Co). The letters here are outlines,
not font files, but using a typeface in a logo still needs a licence that allows
it. Most desktop licences do. If you would rather avoid the question entirely,
Century Gothic is already on your machine and is the closest match to your
original letterforms — say the word and I will rebuild the lockup in it.

## PDF / Illustrator

To get a PDF or `.ai` of the full lockup, place `naledi-art-studio-logo.svg` into
Illustrator and save as. The SVG is plain filled paths with no clip paths,
gradients, embedded images or text, so it imports cleanly.
