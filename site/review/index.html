The diagnosis

Three problems

Not one problem, and none of them the painting’s fault.

01

The cut-out failed

Whoever removed the background left a grey halo around every edge. On white you barely notice. On anything else it shows as a dirty outline.

02

Nothing to enlarge

418 pixels is a thumbnail. Enough for a small website image, nowhere near enough for a banner, a vinyl sign or a printed catalogue.

03

Pieces were missing

The hair and several sunflowers stopped where the eraser stopped, not where the painting did. Shapes ended mid-petal.

11

Colour · how to combine

Two grounds

One accent, used sparingly. Roughly 70 / 22 / 8.

Mostly paper, ink for everything you read, and only a little gold. The gold stops working the moment there is a lot of it. On a dark ground: Night, cream type, the lighter gold for accents, and never pure white, which glares.

Legibility, as numbers

PairingContrastSafe for
Ink on Paper15.5 : 1Anything, any size
Paper on Night17.5 : 1Anything, any size
Plum on Paper12.2 : 1Anything, any size
Sunflower Deep on Paper5.3 : 1Small text and labels
Sunflower on Night7.7 : 1Small text and labels
Sunflower on Paper2.3 : 1Shapes only, never text

That last row is the trap. The bright gold looks right on a pale ground but fails as reading text. Use Sunflower Deep when gold has to be read.

Typeface

Jost Light

The same bones as the letters you were already using, measured rather than guessed.

Naledi

MeasuredYoursJostGotham
Stem weight7.7%7.9%7.1%
Width of NALEDART7.106.637.04

Jost is the closer match on weight, which is the harder thing to fake. Gotham needed a hairline stroke added to reach your 7.7%; Jost arrives there on its own. It sets narrower, so the lockup is tracked out to land on your measured width exactly.

Free to use

Jost is published under the SIL Open Font License. You can use it in the logo, on signage, in documents and on the website, and so can anyone you hand the brand to. No licence to buy, and nothing to renew.

Why it changed

The marks were first built in Gotham Narrow, a commercial face. The files were safe, because the letters are outlines, but setting any fresh text in it would have needed a paid licence. That question is now gone.

Rules that hold it together

Emphasise with weight, italic or colour, never by inventing a sixth size. Labels are uppercase with generous letter-spacing; nothing else is uppercase. Body text stops at about 70 characters a line. One typeface throughout, so nothing can drift apart and there is no licence to manage.

18

Layout

Space

The artwork is busy. Everything around it should not be.

The spacing scale

4, 8, 12, 18, 24, 32, 44, 68, 104. Pick from the scale rather than typing a number. Gaps that are nearly the same read as mistakes.

Margins grow

Phone 18px, tablet 28px, laptop 48px, large screen 72px and above. A margin that stays fixed makes a big screen look like a small one that has been stretched.

Measured text

Images and panels may run the whole width. Paragraphs stop at about 58 characters, headings at about 20. Past that the eye loses its place.

One alignment

Left aligned, ragged right. Centred type only inside a badge or a stamp, where the shape is doing the centring.

Give the page more air than feels necessary and the work carries itself.

19

Photography

The rooms

Warm light, real rooms, people actually doing something.

Do

Daylight where you can. Show the room as it is, with its concrete and its plaster. Let people be mid-action rather than posed at the camera.

Avoid

Heavy filters, cold blue casts, and stock photography of anyone who has never been in the building. The rooms are the proof.

Crop

Wide enough to read the space. A tight crop on a prop tells nobody what they would be hiring.

Room around the mark

Clear space

One cap height all round, and a floor under every mark.

Measure the height of a capital N in the mark. Keep that much empty on every side: no type, no photograph edge, no border. For the round marks, keep a quarter of the ring’s diameter.

Backgrounds

Grounds

Where the mark sits happily, and the one place it does not.

Cream, white, pale warm greys the standard ink set
Deep plum, charcoal, black the reversed cream set
Photographs reversed set, quiet areas only

The one real trap

Her hair is very dark. On a near-black ground the full-colour artwork loses its outline and the head disappears. On dark grounds use the reversed set, or the submark, which carries a ring to hold the edge.

22

Please avoid

Six ways

Six ways to undo all of this, none of them hard to avoid.

01

Stretching it

Scale from a corner, holding proportion. A squashed face is the first thing anyone notices.

02

Recolouring the artwork

The painting stays as painted. Only the lettering and the rule may change colour.

03

Re-typing the words

The spacing is measured, not typed. Use the supplied files rather than setting the name fresh.

04

Adding effects

No drop shadows, outlines, glows or bevels. The artwork already carries its own texture.

05

Cropping the O

The whole artwork sits inside that circle on purpose. A tighter crop cuts the sunflowers again.

06

Rebuilding from a screenshot

That is how we got here. Always start from the supplied SVG or PDF.

Every file was checked to render with no fonts installed, so a printer who owns nothing at all still gets the right letters. The typeface is free anyway.

Digital print, not screen print

2,202 colours cannot be separated into spot inks. For fabric, ask for DTG or DTF.

Convert to CMYK for litho

The files are sRGB. Ask your printer to convert and send a proof. The golds are the ones to check.

Two things still open

Which weight for the classic lockup, 6.5% or 7.7%. And the monogram reads as a word: N beside O can be read as NO. Say the word and I will rework it.

Keeping it consistent

Who decides

One source, one set of checks, and a person to ask.

One source

Every mark comes from one master artwork file. Anything made from a screenshot, a website image or a WhatsApp forward is not the logo.

Sending it out

Printers and signwriters get the PDF or SVG. Anyone designing for you gets the SVG and this document. Nobody needs the PNG unless they ask.

Before it goes out

Check three things: the mark is not stretched, it has its clear space, and the gold is the deep one if it is being read as text.

When in doubt

Ask before you improvise. A five-minute question is cheaper than a reprint, and far cheaper than a sign.
